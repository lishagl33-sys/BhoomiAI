1. Executive Summary

BhoomiVerify is an intelligent, automated end-to-end land record digitization and verification platform designed specifically to address Smart India Hackathon Problem Statement SIH26018. The platform transforms degraded historical paper records, RTCs (Record of Rights, Tenancy and Crops), and Sale Deeds into authoritative, verified digital assets. Combining computer vision, Indic-aware OCR, automated logical consistency validation, duplicate detection, and side-by-side mismatch identification against government reference datasets, BhoomiVerify reduces manual verification times from months to seconds.

2. Problem Statement & Ground Reality

  • Degraded Paper Records: Fragile records subject to tearing, moisture, and ink fade.
  • Multilingual Complexities: Documents alternate between regional languages (Kannada, Marathi, Hindi) and English.
  • Fraudulent Duplicates: Multiple illegal registrations on identical survey numbers.
  • Data Discrepancies: Unmatched ownership names, incorrect land classifications, and conflicting boundary dimensions.
  • Manual Audit Backlogs: Extensive bureaucratic delays and human error in checking cross-departmental records.
